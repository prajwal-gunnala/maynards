04 · Architecture

One word, one pass, three devices.

The planner decides
Live memory, heat, battery and round trip time. One unbroken run of layers each, largest device first.
The helper never sees your prompt
It receives a hidden state, computes its layers and hands the result back. No text ever reaches it.
The head stays home
Embeddings and output head are pinned to the host, which is why only kilobytes cross per word.

05 · Engineering

What made a split usable at all.

Loading
517 s → 70 s
With mmap the engine read the file in scattered pieces while pushing layers out. Read it once, front to back.
A split never used before
437 s → 70 s
Every device keeps its own layers, checked against the engine's own file.
And the rest
  • Embeddings and output head pinned to the hostso one small activation crosses per word, not weights
  • One conversation slota long chat stays in the cache instead of being re-read
  • Median of ten round trips, never the averageone Wi-Fi spike cannot evict a healthy device
  • A sustained run, not a single shot11.4 → 5.3 tok/s as the phone goes 34.6 → 47.5 °C, raw CSV committed
  • Text and vision on two phones at oncerouted by the request, with a header naming who answered
It refuses
The planner says no, in a sentence you can read.
  • Too slow to reach, above 60 ms
  • Too hot, or nearly flat
  • The model already fits one devicesplitting would only make it slower
Seven hermetic tests cover the planner. It will tell you your phone is not worth using, and that is the feature.

08 · Prior art

What already exists, and what it leaves undone.

SystemWhat it doesWhat it leaves to you
DarkbloomEigen Labs, a16z backed Rents idle Apple Silicon Macs for inference and pays their owners One whole model per Mac. It does not pool memory, and never touches a phone
exogithub, 47k stars Splits a model across your own Macs and Linux boxes, by how much memory each has The phone is not a first class device. It does not refuse a bad one, or explain
Petalspeer reviewed A public swarm of volunteer servers holding one very large model 0.83 tokens per second across two continents. Unmaintained since 2024
llama.cpp RPCthe primitive we use Runs layers on another machine over a socket. MeshAI drives this underneath You type the addresses and the tensor split by hand. Nothing decides
Ollama · LM StudioJan, and the rest Excellent single device runtimes. One machine, one model If the model does not fit that machine, you do not run it at all
MeshAIwhat we built An Android app where a phone hosts or helps. A planner reads live memory, heat, battery and link, gives each device layers it can hold, and says no in words Nothing. No paper and no product pools phone memory to run one model
Any device, mixed
Android phones and laptops in one cluster, and any GGUF model. Nothing is hardcoded: we read the header and size it ourselves.
The phone is a peer, not an accessory

09 · The business

Their own devices become the private cloud.

We do not buy hardware and we do not rent anyone else's. We sell the software that turns the devices an organisation already owns into the cluster that runs its own AI.
Who pays

11 · Limits

What we will not claim.

Volunteering these is what makes the rest believable.
Not faster
It buys memory, not speed.
  • Every word crosses the link once
  • A split is slower than one device that already fits
  • Our own planner says so, and refuses your phone
  • We split only when the model fits nowhere else
Not cheaper per token
A commodity API is cheaper. That is not the point.
  • We are for work that is not allowed to leave
  • And for hardware that cannot hold the model alone
  • If your data can leave and you want the best answer, use the cloud
Private, but precisely
Across devices one person owns. No further.
  • Nothing leaves the devices you own
  • The demo runs with the internet off
  • We do not claim activations sent to a stranger are privatepublished work turns them back into text
